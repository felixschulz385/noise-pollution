Barrier audit: device check

This page checks that the audit app will work on this computer. The automatic checks take up to a minute. Then please do the three short steps below the map.

    The report is saved when the checks finish.

    1. Drag the map a little with the mouse or touchpad.

    2. Click on the map, then press the ↑ arrow key once.

    3. Do you see an aerial photo with a red line along a road?
