Barrier audit

    Each task shows one noise barrier on an aerial photo. The line is the barrier's recorded position, which is the centre of the road or track, not the wall.

    Drag the photo (or press ↑/↓) until the line lies on the wall. Match the wall's foot or its shadow line, not its top. Then press Submit (Enter).

    If there is a wall on both sides of the road or railway along this stretch, press Both sides (B) instead of choosing one.

    lines are other barriers in the register, also drawn at their road or track centre. They are for orientation only; answer for the pink line.

    For a road barrier, Street View (G) opens Google Street View in another tab, looking along the barrier: its left is up in this map. Use it when the photo leaves you unsure, then come back and line up the wall here.

    If you can't place it, press Unsure (U) and choose a reason. Nearby tasks come one after another. The first tasks are practice: afterwards the app shows where the wall really is.
