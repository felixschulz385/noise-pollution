**[Florida — `road_projects` source: requirements for implementation](data/florida/road_projects/README.md)**

1. **Historical depth — the central open question, same as `road_network`'s
   item 4 but sharper here.** **Partially resolved 2026-09-14** — checked (a)
   below empirically, changes the scope for the better:
   - **(a) RESOLVED — `Active_Construction_Projects` is NOT current-only
     despite its name.** Queried its live `min`/`max`/`count` statistics on
     `StartDate`: **2,428 total rows, `StartDate` spanning 2009-03-03 to
     2026-04-16.** It retains completed contracts for ~17 years, not just
     presently-active ones — a real, usable window that reaches well past
     the FCAT-2.0 era (2011–2014) and into the 2009-2010 range, materially
     better than the 2019 floor the downloadable historical-archive check
     below implied. Confirmed via `outStatistics` (`min`/`max`/`count` on
     `StartDate`), not by trusting the layer's name.
   - **(b) still open** — `Work_Program_Current`'s 21 phase layers (incl.
     Construction=2, PD&E=13) genuinely are current-window only: a live
     query for `FISCALYR<2020` returned **zero** rows. So the *fiscal-year
     timing/phase* detail these layers add is only available for
     ~2020-onward projects; for older walls, `Active_Construction_Projects`'
     `StartDate`/`EstEndDate`/`Description` (2009+) is the only usable
     signal from the two live services.
   - **(c) still open** — the downloadable historical archive
     (`fdotewp1.dot.state.fl.us`) only reaches adoption year 2019 for the
     Work-Program-style geodatabase snapshots; not yet checked whether an
     FDOT public-records/data request could extend `Work_Program_Current`-
     equivalent detail back past 2009, or whether `Active_Construction_
     Projects`' 2009 floor should just be the source's documented coverage
     boundary (event-study walls before ~2009 would have no road-works
     control from this source — same shape as `road_network`'s own pre-2004
     gap against 1990s barriers). **Recommendation given (a)+(b): build v1
     against `Active_Construction_Projects` as the primary timing/type
     source (2009+, `Description` keyword-scannable for widening/resurfacing/
     wall language) and treat `Work_Program_Current` layers 2/13 as a
     *supplementary* fiscal-year/phase-detail enrichment for 2020+ rows only
     — don't block v1 on resolving (c).**
2. **`RDWYSIDE` on `Work_Program_Current`** — unverified whether this is a
   real carriageway/side attribute. If it is, it would be *new* information
   (`road_network`'s `rciroads` has no side field at all, per that source's

5. **Quieter-pavement / OGFC resurfacing** (Cluster D's fourth row) — neither

   four Cluster D rows per `covariates.md`'s own tags.

**[`noise_barriers`](data/sweden/noise_barriers/README.md)**

For Trafikverket, discussed at a call on 2026-11-25 with their noise and vibration
coordination (slides:
[`2026-11-trafikverket/`](../output/presentations/2026-11-trafikverket/trafikverket-meeting.pdf)).

this page, and the barrier counts in the line documents, are provisional until then.

Barriers and construction:

1. Do older registers or project archives hold heights and construction
   years for the road walls loaded in 2006?
2. Is `built_year` the year a wall was completed, and does rebuilding a
   wall overwrite it with the new year?
3. Are removed walls recorded anywhere? Both exports list only current
   records (every `VALID_TO` is 9999-12-31, checked 2026-11-18). Can older
   versions be ordered, e.g. with an earlier `Betraktelsedatum` on Lastkajen?
4. Is the exact position of each wall available (the side of the road, the
   distance from it), beyond the centreline geometry?
5. Is there any source of start or announcement dates for the road projects
   that came with the walls? The traffic-information API keeps finished
   roadworks for only about a year ([`../covariates.md`](data/sweden/covariates.md),
   Cluster D).
6. Construction years for specific walls: road row 1166 (kommun 1880,
   Örebro) and rail row 551 (kommun 1293, Hässleholm), the two 2001 walls
   that carry the school-level headline, and the undated walls beside the
   58 treated schools dropped for lack of a year
   ([`prespec-construction-years.md`](lines/sweden-schools/prespec-construction-years.md)).
7. Are walls that were planned or proposed but never built recorded anywhere?

Facade measures and context:

8. Is the register of properties considered for noise mitigation (façade
   insulation; the data of Lindgren 2021, "A sound investment?") available to
   researchers: measure carried out, type, year, cost, and the SCB 100 m cell
   (enough; exact positions are not needed)? Who holds it today?
9. Are noise levels calculated for a property or a wall, before and after the
   measure, kept (in the measure's documentation or the strategic noise maps)?
10. Train counts before the walls were built, and road ÅDT for years or
    segments NVDB's history does not cover (our traffic panel matches about
    30–40% of segments, 1998–2019; [`traffic`](data/sweden/traffic/README.md)): which
    years exist?
11. The rules over time: which noise levels qualified a property or a stretch
    for a measure, and since when?
12. Access: the format, the terms, and whether each source needs a data use
    agreement.

**[`road_network`](data/sweden/road_network/README.md)**

Not blocking.

2. **Carriageway-sibling detection is imperfect, not exhaustive.** Only
   ~54% of one-directional nearest-parallel-similar-length matches among
   barrier-touched network rows are reciprocal (checked live); the
   `reciprocal_carriageway_sibling` gate keeps only those, so ~46% of
   candidate pairings are discarded as noise rather than risked. A tighter
   or differently-tuned heuristic (narrower search distance, a length-ratio
   or angle threshold refit against more real examples) could plausibly
   recover some of that -- not attempted, current thresholds were only
   validated against the sample checked this session.
3. No main-line/highway-class filter applied (Florida's `arterial_subset`
   equivalent) -- every current `bilnät` segment is a candidate, including
   minor local roads. The five undownloaded thematic layers (`funkvägklass`
   especially, or `väghållare` for a state-road-only filter) would enable
   this if matching performance or false-positive corridors become a
   concern.
4. Whether NVDB has a genuine route/road-number product (a `Bandel`
   equivalent) was not investigated -- the six-package Sverigefiler list
   checked this session didn't include one, but a different NVDB export
   might.

Not blocking.

   never match a segment within 1000m at all, a spatial-match limit
   separate from the `Betraktelsedatum` question). More orders would raise
   the ceiling further but each additional order has diminishing returns
   now that most 4-year gaps are filled.

   confidence-weighted regression, or just flag `Stickprovsmätning`/
   `Bedömt flöde` rows for a robustness check, not decided.

   8-order union, up from 1994 with 2 orders) -- whether it goes back
   further wasn't tested; an order at an even earlier `Betraktelsedatum`
   (pre-1999) would test this directly, but returns are likely small given
   how little changed between the 1984 floor and the 1999 order.

**[Documentation Conventions](conventions.md)**

A doc's open questions go under one heading, `## Open questions`. When one is answered, it
moves to `## Resolved questions` in the same doc, with the date. A pre-spec's open choices stay
in its `## 0. Decisions for the author`. [`open-questions.md`](open-questions.md) collects both
(generated), so nothing has to be copied there by hand. The heading is matched exactly, and a
section ends at the next heading, so keep its items as a list without subheadings.

**[Per-wall stack and dB dose (Pre-Specification)](lines/florida-schools/prespec-per-wall-stack.md)**

1. **Event window.** Recommended: ±6 years around the wall's construction year. Alternatives:
   ±4 (more walls with a full window), ±8. **Decided:**
2. **Which controls.** Recommended: each wall's own control-band schools only (33 walls / 46
   protected schools as measured 2026-09-30). Widening with band schools of other walls on the
   same `roadway_id` within about 1 km along the road is a robustness check. **Decided:**
3. **Weights.** Recommended: Wing et al. (2024) corrective weights, so the estimate is an
   average per protected school; unweighted OLS as robustness. **Decided:**
4. **Clusters and the deciding test.** Recommended: standard errors clustered by wall (or by
   group of walls that share a school, if any school enters two stacks); RI decides; the wild
   cluster restricted bootstrap by district (as in the notebook's §16) is reported and labels a
   result "fragile" when above 0.05. **Decided:**
5. **Outcome.** Recommended: `z_mss_w` collapsed to the school-year, as in §4. The grade ×
   subject × year cells are a robustness check. **Decided:**
6. **Event-study base period.** Recommended: TWFE event study with t = −1 as reference; for any
   `csdid` event study, `long2`. **Decided:**

**[The 2001 cohort and recovered construction years (Pre-Specification)](lines/sweden-schools/prespec-construction-years.md)**

1. **Which schools get the date check.** Recommended: the barriers of **all 23 in-window
   treated schools** (and the 58 undated ones, block G2), with the same protocol, not only the
   two 2001 schools. Checking only the schools that carry the result is a selective audit: it
   can only remove an effect, never add one. **Decided:**
2. **Which evidence counts for a construction year** (rule ◇ G1). Recommended order: (a) a
   Trafikverket project record or archive; (b) the register's `built_year`; (c) a bracket from
   dated aerial imagery (Lantmäteriet historical orthophotos; Street View for roads), the
   first year the wall is visible, if the bracket is at most 2 years wide. `valid_from` alone
   never counts (the 2006 batch only says "built by 2006"). **Decided:**
3. **Bracket width for imagery dates.** Recommended: at most 2 years (the year assigned is the
   first year the wall is visible). Alternative: 3 years, which recovers more schools with a
   noisier event time. **Decided:**
4. **The parental-education gradient.** Recommended: re-test it **only on newly identifying
   schools** (schools that gain a pre- and post-period through this step). They did not
   produce the gradient, so this is a confirmatory test. The full-sample estimate is reported
   but decides nothing. Alternative: re-test on the full corrected sample (more power, but it
   contains the 11 schools that produced the gradient). **Decided:**
5. **Event-study base period.** Recommended: adopt `long2` for `estat event` (every pre-period
   relative to g−1, as a textbook event study), in this line and in Florida's. It changes only
   the pre-period plot; `estat simple` and the post-period ATTs do not change. **Decided:**
6. **Outcome.** Recommended: `meritvärde_z` stays the outcome. SALSA's residual, pass rate and
   eligibility are secondary and decide nothing. **Decided:**
7. **Multiple testing across the two deciding tests** (◇ A1, ◇ B1). Recommended: Holm over
   the two. **Decided:**
